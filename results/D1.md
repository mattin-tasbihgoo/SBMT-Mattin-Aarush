# D1: inference and post-processing options, chosen by cross-fitting (2026-11-16)

Inference only, using the 5 E0 fold models; no training. Pre-registered in `results/RUNS.md`
(commit `15a3aa5`) before any scoring. Code: `src/evaluation/infer_variants_cv.py` and
`src/evaluation/d1_crossfit.py`. Files are in `results/d1_2026-11-16/`:

- `crossfit_choices.csv`: the choice per fold;
- `per_case_crossfit.csv`: all metrics per subject;
- `summary.csv`;
- `ship_rule.csv`;
- `grid_dice_long.csv.gz`: Dice for every subject × configuration;
- `pooled_config_means_descriptive.csv`: descriptive only.

Quote as: **"5-fold CV, out-of-fold, n = 484, E0 + D1 inference, 2026-11-16"**.

## 1. What was tested

The grid has 6 inference variants × 4 thresholds × 7 post-processing rules = 168 configurations.

**Inference variants** (each subject predicted only by the model of its own test fold):

| ID | Weighting | Overlap | Flip TTA |
|---|---|---|---|
| I0 (E0) | count map | 50% | no |
| I1 | Gaussian | 50% | no |
| I2 | count map | 50% | yes |
| I3 | Gaussian | 50% | yes |
| I4 | count map | 75% | no |
| I5 | Gaussian | 75% | no |

**Thresholds:** 0.3, 0.4, 0.5, 0.6.

**Post-processing rules:**

- none;
- largest connected component;
- drop components smaller than 100, 1,000 or 5,000 voxels;
- keep components at least 10% or 25% of the largest one's size.

**Cross-fitting:** each fold's configuration was chosen by mean Dice on the other four folds
only.

**Inference check:** inference was re-run for I0 and matched the stored E0 maps to within
1.7e-3 in probability (GPU nondeterminism). The baseline uses the stored E0 maps, and its
per-case Dice matches E0 to 5e-7.

## 2. The choice

All five folds independently chose the **same** configuration:

> **I2 (flip TTA, count averaging, 50% overlap), threshold 0.5, keep every connected component
> at least 10% the size of the largest.**

On the other folds it beat the E0 baseline by +0.0050 to +0.0085. The runner-up was always
within 0.0005: the same TTA with a minimum-size rule, or 75% overlap.

## 4. Where the gain comes from (descriptive, pooled; not used for any choice)

| Mean Dice, threshold 0.5 | Largest CC | No post-processing | Keep ≥ 10% of largest |
|---|---|---|---|
| I0, no TTA | 0.8972 | 0.9015 | 0.9019 |
| I2, flip TTA | 0.8983 | 0.9030 | **0.9036** |
| I1, Gaussian | 0.8968 | 0.9005 | 0.9013 |
| I4, 75% overlap | 0.8978 | 0.9029 | 0.9031 |

- **Dropping "largest component only" gives about three quarters of the gain** (+0.0047).
  - It restores correctly found second lesions: BRATS_258 0.635 → 0.895, BRATS_125 0.674 → 0.906,
    BRATS_292 0.707 → 0.912, BRATS_154 0.786 → 0.937, plus BRATS_364, 123, 066 and 019.
  - The 10%-of-largest rule still removes small stray false positives.
- **Flip TTA adds +0.0017.** Gaussian weighting does not help this model (slightly worse
  everywhere). 75% overlap is about as good as TTA but was not chosen.
- **Threshold 0.5 stays best.** 0.4 and 0.6 are each worse by about 0.001.

## 5. What got worse

Eleven cases lose more than 0.005 Dice. The largest losses:

| Subject | E0 | E0 + D1 | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| BRATS_477 | 0.921 | 0.802 | −0.119 |
| BRATS_132 | 0.901 | 0.845 | −0.055 |
| BRATS_428 | 0.864 | 0.813 | −0.051 |
| BRATS_168 | 0.867 | 0.821 | −0.047 |

These are cases where a false-positive second region is now kept because it is at least 10% of
the largest. They are listed for the next error review, not tuned on.

The persistent failures barely move:

| Subject | E0 | E0 + D1 |
|---|---|---|
| BRATS_381 | 0.521 | 0.546 |

They are model or label problems, not post-processing ones. They need step E (training
changes; for example the edema misses suggest adding T2) or label review.

## 6. Caveats

- **The candidate list was not blind.** It was written after E0's pooled table showed that
  largest-CC hurts (disclosed in the pre-registration). Cross-fitting keeps each fold's choice
  blind to that fold's subjects.
- **The cross-fitting penalty is near zero here.** All folds made the same choice, and it is
  also the pooled best, so the selection penalty that cross-fitting guards against is about 0.
- **New baseline.** E0 + D1 (this inference configuration) is now the baseline that step E
  variants are compared against, with bottom decile **0.7542**. Each E variant uses this
  inference configuration fixed, unless it re-runs D1 by cross-fitting.
- **Not comparable** with BraTS/MSD leaderboard numbers (whole tumor only, different data
  splits).
